Chemical Discoveries

The Perseverance rover has determended that the Jezero Crater is chemically and mineralogically diverse, composed primarily of igneous floor rocks rich in olivine and pyroxene, alongside extensive water-altered clay minerals, carbonates, and sulfates. The crater also has trace amounts of CO2
