Diagnosing Windows XP Display Issues

The new "Luna" visual style in Windows XP demands more from your video card. If you are experiencing a blank screen, distorted colors, or resolution problems, follow these steps to restore your display.

  1. Verify Physical Monitor Connections
    Ensure the computer is completely turned off. Disconnect the video cable (typically a blue VGA connector) from the back of the computer and the monitor, check for any bent pins, and firmly reconnect it. Tighten the thumbscrews to secure the connection, and ensure the monitor's power cable is plugged into a working outlet.

  2. Boot the System into Safe Mode
    Restart the computer and rapidly tap the F8 key before the Windows logo appears. Use the arrow keys to select Safe Mode from the Advanced Boot Options menu and press Enter. This will load a basic, low-resolution desktop.

  3. Utilize the Driver Rollback Feature
    While in Safe Mode, click the Start button, right-click My Computer, and select Properties. Navigate to the Hardware tab and click Device Manager. Expand "Display adapters," right-click your video card, and select Properties. Go to the Driver tab and click Roll Back Driver to revert to the previous working state.

  4. Adjust Display Settings to Safe Defaults
    If the image is visible but distorted, right-click anywhere on the empty desktop and select Properties. Click the Settings tab. Drag the "Screen resolution" slider down to 800 by 600 pixels, and set the "Color quality" drop-down to Medium (16-bit). Click Apply.
