Connecting to the Internet in Windows XP

Getting online with Microsoft's upcoming Windows XP operating system is expected to be significantly more streamlined than in Windows 98 or ME, thanks to a redesigned interface and robust built-in networking tools. Whether you are using a standard 56k dial-up modem or upgrading to a high-speed DSL or cable connection, here is how you will connect to the World Wide Web in the new OS.

Click the new Start button and select the Control Panel. In the updated Category View, click on "Network and Internet Connections," and then select Network Connections.

Select "Connect to the Internet" and click Next. You will be prompted to set up your connection manually or use a CD from your Internet Service Provider (ISP). If setting up manually, choose either a dial-up modem (you will need your ISP's phone number, username, and password) or a broadband connection that is always on.

Once the wizard finishes and your modem dials in, double-click the "" icon on your desktop to launch Internet Explorer 6.
