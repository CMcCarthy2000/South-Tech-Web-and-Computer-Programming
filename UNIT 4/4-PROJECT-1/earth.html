Earth


Distance from Sun: 1.00 AU Moons: 1 (The Moon)
Orbital Period: 365.25 Days Rotation Period: 24 Hours

Earth is the third planet from the Sun and the only world known to support liquid surface oceans and life. Its atmosphere consists primarily of nitrogen and oxygen, protecting the surface from solar radiation.
