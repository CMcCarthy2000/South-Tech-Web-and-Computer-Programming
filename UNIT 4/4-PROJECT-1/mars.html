Mars


Distance from Sun: 1.52 AU Moons: 2 (Phobos, Deimos)
Orbital Period: 687 Earth Days Rotation Period: 24.6 Hours

Mars is the fourth planet from the Sun. Known as the Red Planet due to iron oxide on its surface, Mars features polar ice caps, vast canyon systems, and Olympus Mons, the largest volcano in the solar system.
