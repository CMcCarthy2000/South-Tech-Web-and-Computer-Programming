Mercury


Distance from Sun: 0.39 AUMoons: 0
Orbital Period: 88 Earth DaysRotation: 58.6 Earth Days

Mercury is the innermost planet in our solar system. It features a heavily cratered metallic surface similar to Earth's Moon and experiences extreme surface temperature variations between day and night.
