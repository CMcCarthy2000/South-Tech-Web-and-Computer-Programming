Neptune


Distance from Sun: 30.07 AU Moons: 8+ (Triton)
Orbital Period: 164.8 Earth Years Rotation Period: 16.1 Hours

Neptune is the eighth planet from the Sun and the outermost ice giant in our solar system. Known for its vivid blue color caused by atmospheric methane, Neptune experiences the fastest recorded wind speeds of any planet and features a dynamic atmosphere imaged during Voyager 2's 1989 flyby.
