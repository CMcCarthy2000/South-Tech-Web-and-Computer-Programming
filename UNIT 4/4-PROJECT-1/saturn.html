Saturn


Distance from Sun: 9.54 AU Moons: 18+ (Titan, Enceladus)
Orbital Period: 29.45 Earth Years Rotation Period: 10.7 Hours

Saturn is the sixth planet from the Sun and the second-largest in the solar system. Famous for its magnificent, wide ring system composed primarily of ice and rock particles, Saturn is a gas giant surrounded by a complex moon system including cloud-covered Titan.
