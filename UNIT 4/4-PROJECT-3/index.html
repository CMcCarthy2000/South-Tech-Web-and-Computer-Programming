Earth Orbiting Civilian Station

This webpage is for the Earth Orbiting Civilian Station.
What is the Earth Orbiting Civilian Station you may ask? The station will be able to hold up too 20 personel who will be a mix of normal civilians and trained personel for up too 1 year.

The station will be constructed off of the International Space Station and then detached from it and boosted into a higher orbit to reduce the amount of fuel it needs to cary onboard for orbital corrections.

The first module of the station is predicted to launch abord STS-140 sometime in January of 2014 with later modules launched from the Baikonur cosmodrome via the Soyuz Rocket later the same month.
